# Industry pathway images

Generated with the built-in image generation tool for the academy homepage.
These images illustrate industry possibilities; they do not depict actual
AeroCADmy learners, facilities, or completed projects.

## Shared generation prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: production website image atlas for
AeroCADmy drone technology education. Generate a LANDSCAPE contact sheet of EXACTLY
FOUR separate realistic documentary photographs in a precise 2 by 2 grid. Edge to
edge panels, absolutely no gutters, no borders, no text labels, no headings, no
watermarks, no graphical UI overlays. Each quadrant is a complete independent
photograph, not a blended collage. Overall aspect ratio 3:2, each quadrant 3:2.
Insert the four scenes below in top-left, top-right, bottom-left, bottom-right order.
Photography must feel authentic and premium with natural soft light, believable
machinery and hands, muted greens, navy workwear, neutral warm highlights,
consistent colour grade. Indian context where people appear, documentary candid
framing, often viewed from behind or hands only; do not depict branded AeroCADmy
students or facilities. Educational illustration of industry possibilities, no
career promises. Computers can show plausible maps, code or imagery but no legible
text is required. No cartoon, no 3D render, no sci-fi drones, no neon, no decorative
icons. Strict precise equally-sized 2x2 photographs, no padding at outer edges.
